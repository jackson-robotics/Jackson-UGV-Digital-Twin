Experimental design:
  OFAT local sensitivity analysis, 15 runs per condition, 105 total runs.
  Perturbation bands are controlled sensitivity perturbations and are NOT empirical confidence intervals of calibration uncertainty.

Trajectory deviation:
  Arc-length-weighted RMS nearest-point distance from each aligned GT trajectory to the calibrated C00 median GT polyline. C00 uses leave-one-out references to quantify intrinsic repeatability.

Obstacle clearance:
  Minimum post-insertion planar geometric separation between the cylinder and the conservative audited USD robot collision envelope.

Navigation time:
  Maximum NavigateToPose feedback.navigation_time, using simulation time.

Statistics:
  Continuous metrics are summarized for SUCCEEDED runs using median [IQR] as primary descriptors because several conditions show skewed or multimodal behavior.
  All runs, including the single ABORTED run, contribute to success rate.
