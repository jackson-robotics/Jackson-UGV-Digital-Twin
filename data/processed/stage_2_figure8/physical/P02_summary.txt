Publication-ready result paragraph
-----------------------------------
The physical Jackson platform successfully completed all 10 figure-eight trials. The mean EKF-derived closure error was 6.90 +/- 0.73 cm, whereas the independently measured physical closure error was 11.59 +/- 3.14 cm. The final absolute heading drift remained limited to 1.42 +/- 0.22 deg, and the cumulative rotational discrepancy was 5.29 +/- 0.67 deg. These results indicate repeatable rotational tracking while also revealing a systematic difference between EKF-estimated and physically observed translational closure.
